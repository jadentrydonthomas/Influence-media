# Webull vs the World — Retail Broker Growth 2022 → mid-2026

An interactive, self-contained dashboard comparing **Webull** against **Robinhood**
and the other fastest-moving retail brokers worldwide (Trade Republic, Interactive
Brokers, moomoo/Futu, eToro, Trading 212), plus the incumbents they're chasing.

Open **`index.html`** in any browser — no build step, no network calls, works offline.

## What's inside

- **World heat map** with three modes:
  1. *Webull growth heat* — where Webull's growth actually is (core vs expanding vs newest markets).
  2. *Fastest-growing challenger* — which of the seven tracked challengers leads each market.
  3. *Competition density* — how crowded each market is.
- **The growth race** — funded/active accounts 2022→mid-2026, shown both in absolute
  millions and indexed to 100 at end-2022 (the growth-rate view).
- **Growth-rate bars** — latest YoY account growth, asset growth, and total customer assets.
- **Country cards** — 14 markets, Webull vs the local competition (challengers colored,
  incumbents in gray), each with a short read on the local dynamic.
- **Expansion timelines** — Webull's map-first vs Robinhood's product-first strategy.
- **An honest conclusion** — who's growing most on each axis, and a clear-eyed take on
  the "$7 Robinhood / Webull has more upside" thesis.

## Headline findings (data as of 13 Jul 2026)

| Axis | Winner | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Most **absolute** new users | **Robinhood** | +1.8M funded customers in 2025 alone |
| Fastest **user %** growth | **IBKR (+34%) · moomoo (+33%) · Trade Republic (~10M)** | not Webull (+8%), not Robinhood (+6%) |
| Fastest **asset** growth | **Webull** | +90% YoY customer assets, +91% net deposits |
| Biggest **white space** | **India & the EU** | 190M+ Indian demat accounts; EU participation structurally rising |

**Bottom line:** trading is becoming culture globally — but the purest beneficiaries
today are Trade Republic (Europe), IBKR and Futu/moomoo (Asia-Pacific). Robinhood is
the profit machine of the trend; Webull is the leveraged, faster-asset-growth call
option on it going international — real upside if its ~760K (and fast-growing)
non-US funded accounts keep compounding and Europe expands beyond the Netherlands,
but +8% account growth does not "pass Robinhood" at current rates.

## Method & honesty notes

- "Users" are compared as **funded/active accounts** (each broker's own definition,
  labeled throughout). Registered-user counts are shown only where flagged — they
  are much larger and not comparable (e.g. Webull 27.6M registered vs 5.1M funded).
- Figures marked **≈** are estimates triangulated from company statements, filings
  and press coverage; year-end values unless noted.
- **No broker discloses city-level user data.** Country is the finest honest
  resolution; app-download rankings are the usual sub-national proxy and are
  directionally consistent with the map.
- The "fastest-growing challenger" map is a judgment call from the cited growth
  data, limited to the seven tracked challengers; local incumbents (XP, Groww,
  Wealthsimple, etc.) appear in the country cards instead.
- **Nothing here is investment advice.**
